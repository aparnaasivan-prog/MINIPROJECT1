Objective of the Dataset
The dataset is designed to provide a comprehensive foundation for analyzing multinational company (MNC) performance across multiple dimensions. It serves as the raw data source for building dashboards, reports, and insights.

Data Cleaning & Transformation
	The dataset was cleaned by handling duplicates, missing values, inconsistencies, invalid dates and data-type issues. A structured data model was then created by establishing relationships between the tables and developing key measures to support interactive analysis and dashboard reporting
  
Technology Stack
•	Excel for data preparation
•	 DAX for analytical modeling
•	Power BI for visualization


Project Overview: MNC Global Analysis Dashboard
This project provides a comprehensive business intelligence dashboard for analyzing multinational company (MNC) performance across global operations. It integrates insights on sales, profitability, employee distribution, and product performance to support data-driven decision-making.


Module	Description
MNC Global Analysis	Summarizes total employees, companies, sales, and profit. Highlights top-performing companies and revenue by country and industry.
Employee & Company Analysis	Examines workforce distribution, salary trends, departmental roles, and global payroll mapping.
Sales & Profitability	Tracks total sales, profit, quantity sold, and loss transactions. Includes payment method analysis and global profit leaders.

Insights
•	Global Reach: Operations span across North America, Europe, Asia, and Australia.
•	Top Performers: Samsung, Wipro, and Sony lead in both sales and profitability.
•	Financial Health: Total sales of 2.48B and total profit of 119.77M, with consistent monthly growth.
•	Workforce Strength: 10K employees with an average salary of 136K, showing balanced departmental distribution.
•	Product Success: Cloud services and analytics platforms drive major revenue streams.

Recommendations
•	Optimize underperforming regions (Australia, Canada) through targeted strategies.
•	Reduce loss transactions via improved quality control and customer engagement.
•	Invest in employee training and retention to sustain productivity.
•	Expand high-profit product lines (Analytics, Cloud Services)
